Frequently asked questions

Why can't I get onto the Minecraft server

If you cannot get onto the Minecraft server, please make sure the server is online and you have typed in the IP correctly. The player count may also be maxed out.

Why is the server sometimes offline?

The server may sometimes be offline for short periods of time during maintenance or the daily restart. At around 08:30 GMT, the server restarts to clear lag.

How can I get VIP or a higher rank?

We only offer higher ranks to players who have been playing for a long time, even during the alpha stages of the server. Moderator roles are also sometimes given out through the Discord.
